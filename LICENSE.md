### Legal Claims:

1. **A switchless, asynchronous computing interconnect architecture comprising:**
   * a transmitter matrix configured to split a digital outbound data stream into a plurality of $N$ parallel independent bitstreams and modulate each bitstream onto a unique, orthogonal carrier frequency chosen from a set of frequencies ($f_1 \dots f_N$);
   * an unrouted physical transport waveguide coupled to said transmitter matrix, configured to concurrently propagate the modulated carrier frequencies within a shared physical medium using frequency-domain multiplexing;
   * a receiver matrix coupled to said transport waveguide, comprising a plurality of $N$ parallel micro-receivers, wherein each individual micro-receiver contains an analog bandpass filtering component hardwired to resonate at a specific designated carrier frequency within said set ($f_1 \dots f_N$) to isolate and capture its corresponding bitstream without digital routing overhead; and
   * an asynchronous buffer array comprising a plurality of self-timed FIFO memories coupled to the outputs of said receiver matrix, configured to ingest the isolated bitstreams and decouple the clock domains of the transmitter matrix from the destination endpoint clock domains.

2. **The interconnect architecture of claim 1, further comprising an active thermal stabilization subsystem, wherein each individual micro-receiver's analog bandpass filtering component is structurally integrated with:**
   * an analog monitor diode configured to output an electrical feedback signal proportional to the real-time wavelength drift of the filtering component; and
   * a localized micro-heater element governed by a closed-loop analog operational amplifier loop coupled to said monitor diode, wherein said operational amplifier loop dynamically alters current to the micro-heater element to counteract transient thermal fluctuations ($\Delta T_{\text{GPU}}$) generated by adjacent computing logic, thereby locking the resonance profile of the filtering component to its designated carrier frequency.

3. **The interconnect architecture of claim 1, wherein said unrouted physical transport waveguide features a high-dispersion geometry engineered to enforce a velocity differential between adjacent carrier frequencies propagating through the medium, thereby suppressing non-linear Four-Wave Mixing (FWM) products and maintaining channel signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratios (SINR).**

4. **The interconnect architecture of claim 1, wherein said transmitter matrix is coupled to a hardware polarization-division multiplexer configured to segregate the plurality of $N$ carrier frequencies into a first subset formatted in a Transverse Electric (TE) polarization state and a second subset formatted in a Transverse Magnetic (TM) polarization state prior to injection into the unrouted physical transport waveguide.**

5. **The interconnect architecture of claim 1, further comprising a hardware-level adaptive remapping matrix positioned downstream of said asynchronous buffer array, configured to execute a Power-On Self-Test (POST) routine to detect physically degraded or non-resonant carrier channels, flag $K$ channels as disabled, and dynamically route the data payload over $M$ pre-fabricated physical spare channels by remapping the hardware memory addresses directly beneath an L1 cache or memory-mapped core configuration.**
